# ZERA public search baseline — 2026-10-24
Pilot: ZERA-DE-001

## Scope
Exploratory public web search; not a live Google ranking measurement, Google Business API read, or an AI-engine response test. Search-engine retrieval is not equivalent to verified attribution or a reproducible GEO score.

## Search probes conducted
- `"ZERA Clothing" "Wetzlar" "ze-ra.de"`
- `site:instagram.com/zeraclothing.official ZERA Clothing Wetzlar`
- `"ZERA Clothing" Wetzlar Google Business boutique`

## Findings
1. **Confirmed search-name ambiguity in the general web corpus:** results include other similarly named clothing businesses and brands outside Germany. Examples: ZERA XCLUSIVE (Netherlands) https://www.zeraxclusive.com/ and Zera Fashion (Baku) https://pinkbazaar.in/store/zera-fashion-8086/. These are **not verified as related to ZERA Clothing in Wetzlar**.
2. **Exact ZERA Clothing + Wetzlar web search:** the exploratory results did not provide an unambiguously verified official business website. **Do not interpret missing retrieved results as non-indexing or absent Google Maps listings.**
3. **Official domain ownership:** ze-ra.de remains unconfirmed as an official business website; email domain does not establish website status.
4. **Instagram baseline:** owner-provided screenshots only (3,575 followers / 142 posts); not a live Instagram API measurement.

## Recommendations, not yet implemented
- Ask the business to confirm its own official website, Google Business Profile URL, address and product categories before publishing company metadata.
- Establish and consistently use unambiguous `ZERA Clothing – Wetzlar` brand descriptors in approved profiles.
- Run reproducible, timestamped provider-level German and Turkish search tests and AI engine mention tests once adapters are connected and approved.
- Do not infer lost sales or visibility uplift from this preliminary finding.
