2. No impersonation, login scraping, password requests, unsolicited mass email, or private analytics.
3. Any account connections, customer information, publication of case-study results, or site changes: separate explicit approval.
4. Add tracking links ONLY to media/assets owned by/authorized by ZERA; secure opt-in analytics and disclosure.
5. Distinguish tracked referrals, verified orders, and causal incremental lift.
6. No recurring fees, deployment, or automatic outreach without owner approval.
