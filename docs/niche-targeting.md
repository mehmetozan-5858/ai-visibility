# Specialist prospect targeting

Four of every five hourly slots prioritize specialist firms in any sector. The rotating CNC, machinery, parts, packaging, laboratory, certification and consulting themes are starting points, not an exhaustive whitelist. One slot retains broad exploration. An explicit manual sector choice is respected.

Focused discovery returns up to twelve verified candidates, accepting fewer rather than padding. Each candidate must include a specific speciality and buyer description plus an HTTPS page on its official business domain. That discovery evidence is retained in the prospect source for subsequent analysis; it is not a completed visibility measurement or an independently established accreditation/export claim.

Specialist candidates get priority before existing qualification scores in analysis and first-contact queues. Sector preference contributes to commercial suitability only. Provider analysis and official contact verification remain separate gates; claims about rankings, citations, traffic, accreditation and revenue require evidence. Existing prospects and reply/follow-up history remain intact. Sending caps remain three per cycle and twenty per Istanbul day.
